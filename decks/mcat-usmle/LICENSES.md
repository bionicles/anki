# Credits and reuse

The combined medical study package includes work with different licenses. Distribute the compiled collection and OpenStax adaptations for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, preserve these credits, and indicate changes. Each packaged note identifies its source and license. [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

**OpenStax, Rice University, and the contributing authors** provide the selected glossary definitions and textbook exercises. The adaptation selects chapters and terms, converts the markup to Anki fields, writes recall prompts for definitions, and maps questions to exam sections. The source revisions and section locators are in the note backs and source lock. The prefaces list the full writing and review teams.

**Histology images** are credited in the OpenStax figure captions to the Regents of the University of Michigan Medical School. The nine crops remove labels or isolate the tissue being tested. Each image card links to the exact textbook figure and carries the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 attribution. To reproduce a crop, use its JPEG from the pinned book’s media directory. Multiply the left and right fractions by the original width and the top and bottom fractions by the original height, round to the nearest pixel, crop, then thumbnail to fit 720 × 520. **Diagnostic imaging** comes from 154 individually credited images, including dermatology photographs, radiographs, CT, MRI, ultrasound, endoscopy, histology, blood and bone marrow films, gross specimens and clinical ECGs on Wikimedia Commons. Each figure retains the license given by its contributor. The ledger identifies the original file, contributor, source URL, license, original file SHA-1, optional crop and output dimensions. To reproduce an image, verify the original checksum, apply the fractional crop if present and resize proportionally to the bounds listed for that image. Twenty-eight monochrome diagnostic images use grayscale PNGs. Another 23 images use optimized RGB PNGs. The decoded RGB values match the preceding versions pixel for pixel. The two color colon micrographs by Nephron show collagenous and lymphocytic colitis. The resized images retain CC BY-SA 3.0. The image credit also appears on every card that uses it.
