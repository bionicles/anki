# Credits and reuse

The combined medical study package includes work with different licenses. Distribute the compiled collection and OpenStax adaptations for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, preserve these credits, and indicate changes. Each packaged note identifies its source and license. [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

**OpenStax, Rice University, and the contributing authors** provide the selected glossary definitions and textbook exercises. The adaptation selects chapters and terms, converts the markup to Anki fields, writes recall prompts for definitions, and maps questions to exam sections. The source revisions and section locators are in the note backs and source lock. The prefaces list the full writing and review teams.

**Histology images** are credited in the OpenStax figure captions to the Regents of the University of Michigan Medical School. The nine crops remove labels or isolate the tissue being tested. Each image card links to the exact textbook figure and carries the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 attribution. To reproduce a crop, use its JPEG from the pinned book’s media directory. Multiply the left and right fractions by the original width and the top and bottom fractions by the original height, round to the nearest pixel, crop, then thumbnail to fit 720 × 520. [Crop coordinates](specimens.tsv) · [Pinned source media](https://github.com/openstax/osbooks-anatomy-physiology/tree/f273762340768a5f50e833c876e40b5a9c565da1/media)

**CDC pathology photographs** come from the CDC’s Public Health Image Library and are available free from the CDC. Images 61, 71, 846, 861, 862, 863 and 865 are credited to Edwin P. Ewing, Jr., MD. Image 23482 is credited to Rosalie B. Haraszti, MD. Image 21261 is credited to CDC. The packaged PNGs resize the public-domain photographs to fit 750 × 570. Every corresponding card includes a direct link to its individual PHIL record and the provider credit. [CDC Public Health Image Library](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/)

**ECG recordings** are from George Moody and Roger Mark, MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, version 1.0.0, PhysioNet, 2005. The eight figures plot six-second excerpts of modified lead II. The card backs identify the record and time interval. To reproduce the strips, download the .hea and .dat files for records 100, 119, 202, 207, 228 and 231 from the dataset. From this deck directory run `uv run --no-project --with wfdb==4.3.0 --with matplotlib==3.10.8 python ecg.py /path/to/records`. The source database is available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. Original hypothetical clinical prompts accompanying the recordings are by this project. [Dataset and attribution](https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/) · [Moody and Mark, IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Magazine, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1109/51.932724) · [ODC-By 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/)
