# Credits and reuse

The combined medical study package includes work with different licenses. Distribute the compiled collection and OpenStax adaptations for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, preserve these credits, and indicate changes. Each packaged note identifies its source and license. [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

**OpenStax, Rice University, and the contributing authors** provide the selected glossary definitions and psychology exercises. The adaptation selects chapters and terms, converts the markup to Anki fields, writes recall prompts for definitions, and maps questions to exam sections. The source revisions and section locators are in the note backs and source lock. The prefaces list the full writing and review teams.
