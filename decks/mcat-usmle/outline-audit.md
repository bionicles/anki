# Final outline audit · 27 November 2026

**The 27,363-card package has broad coverage of the public outlines. Exam readiness and completeness at the individual question level remain unverified.** This audit checked the released package and coverage tables against the official public documents. It added no cards. The 2,097 USMLE topics and 1,669 MCAT decisions are editorial subdivisions. Neither exam publisher sets a requirement for a second case pair or a second three-card sequence on every subdivision.

| Exam | What the comparison verified | What remains unverified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MCAT · 6,250 cards | All four sections have cards. The 31 numbered science category IDs extracted from the AAMC PDF exactly match the 31 in the release. All four science reasoning skills and all three CARS reasoning categories have linked questions. All 1,669 indexed science decisions have a pair of cases with changed evidence. | The deck has 40 CARS passages and 223 questions. Only 13 passages fall within AAMC’s typical 500–600 words, and 27 are shorter. The deck has no timed four-section performance result. The official category match does not verify every possible application. |
| Step 1 · 7,146 cards | The map represents all 18 areas in the common USMLE outline and the four weighted Step 1 physician competency groups. All 2,097 indexed topics have a linked foundation, application and changed case. | The official discipline table lists 11 overlapping disciplines. Nutrition is the only one independently mapped on the deck’s discipline axis. For example, the presence of physiology and pathology cards does not quantify coverage against their official bands. |
| Step 2 CK · 6,674 cards | All 18 common outline areas and all ten physician task groups with nonzero weights have mappings. All 2,097 indexed topics have a linked three-card sequence. | The scorecard does not independently classify all cards against the official medicine, pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, psychiatry and surgery percentages. The number of flashcards in a section cannot establish that distribution. |
| Step 3 · 7,293 cards | The release includes Foundations of Independent Practice, Advanced Clinical Medicine and 660 case-simulation study cards. Every indexed clinical topic has a sequence. All 169 simulation headings contain both follow-up cards from at least one reviewed comparison. | USMLE assesses actions, sequencing, timing and location of care as a case evolves. Independently scheduled cards do not exercise order entry or advancing time in the CCS interface. No simulator performance was measured. |

The extra-depth counts are **101 of 2,097** named topics with a second distinct three-card objective on **all three** Steps, and **465 of 1,669** MCAT science decisions with two pairs using four distinct cards. A topic outside one of these counts still has the baseline practice described above. Each Step also has additional second objectives that do not yet meet the three-Step intersection. USMLE explicitly describes its disease examples as illustrative and says exam questions can include other conditions. [USMLE common outline, introduction and contents](https://www.usmle.org/sites/default/files/2022-01/USMLE_Content_Outline_0.pdf)

**Stopping point.** The current release passed a fresh Anki import and an upgrade from the preceding package with all 27,121 previous card identities and schedules preserved. The outline review ends here. Further expansion would need a new, bounded request or evidence of a specific learning gap.

The MCAT comparison used the numbered science category headings and the CARS specifications in the public outline. The release tables identify the card IDs and passage lengths behind the counts.
